Social media and LinkedIn strategy

Make Shft-In feel like the best part of moving to a new city.

A practical, content-led plan to build awareness, community, trust and enquiries. The focus is on what a Marketing Executive can plan and execute through Instagram, LinkedIn, resident stories and local partnerships.

The big idea

Shft-In should not look like a page that only lists rooms. It should show the lifestyle of a young person starting a new chapter in Gurgaon or Noida: a comfortable space, an easier routine, good people and a city life they can enjoy.

Content rule: Use actual property footage, real residents with consent, team members and verified property details. Show the experience honestly. This will make the brand more trustworthy than heavily staged content.

Role of each platform

Instagram

Make people want to live there

Instagram is the lifestyle, discovery and engagement channel. Show rooms, routines, friendships, local life and relatable moving-city moments through short videos and Stories.

  • Primary goal: reach, saves, shares, DMs and property enquiries.
  • Best formats: Reels, Story polls, resident reposts, carousels and collab posts.
  • Brand feeling: young, warm, aspirational, social and easy.
LinkedIn

Make people trust the brand

LinkedIn is the credibility and partnership channel. Speak to working professionals, HR teams, companies hiring in NCR, homeowners and future partners.

  • Primary goal: professional trust, corporate stays, partnerships and employer brand.
  • Best formats: founder posts, customer stories, carousels, employee content and industry opinions.
  • Brand feeling: helpful, professional, modern and customer-first.

Campaigns to run

01

New City, New Start

A campaign for people relocating for a first job, internship, college or career move. Show both the stress of moving and the excitement of finding a comfortable place.

Content: “3 things nobody tells you before moving to Gurgaon.”
02

Meet Your Neighbours

Feature one resident every week with quick questions: where are you from, what do you do, what is your favourite part of the home and what is your favourite place nearby?

Content: “Meet Riya. She moved from Jaipur to Gurgaon for her first job.”
03

Shft-In Stories

Short, real resident stories that show emotional value, not only the physical room. Keep them natural, personal and simple.

Content: “A room is a room. But home is late-night chai with people who get you.”
04

Shft-In Local Guide

Own the neighbourhood with useful, saveable guides on cafés, commute, grocery stores, gyms, food and weekend plans near each property.

Content: “5 useful places near Shft-In [Location] for your first week in the city.”
05

PG Problems, Solved

Use light humour around common rental and PG frustrations, then make Shft-In the natural lifestyle alternative.

Content: “Expectation vs reality when you move into a regular PG.”
06

Resident Referral Moments

Celebrate residents who introduce friends to the community. Feature move-ins, welcome notes and friend groups, subject to consent and approved referral terms.

Content: “POV: your friend moved in and now your city feels more like home.”

LinkedIn weekly rhythm

Three useful posts

  • One founder, company or industry insight.
  • One resident, employee or customer story.
  • One relocation, community-living or partnership-focused carousel.
Story ideas

Daily interaction

  • “Which room corner would you choose?” poll.
  • “Ask us anything about moving to Gurgaon.” Q&A.
  • Resident reposts and event snippets.
  • Verified availability or visit-booking reminders.

Community and partnership ideas

Offline moments to create

Make the community visible

  • Chai evenings, game nights, movie nights and festive gatherings.
  • Fitness mornings, yoga sessions or small wellness challenges.
  • Room décor contests and roommate cooking challenges.
  • New-resident welcome moments and community introductions.
Local collaborations

Make living easier

  • Partner with nearby cafés, gyms, salons, food brands and coworking spaces.
  • Create resident-only offers after confirming the partner terms.
  • Work with micro-creators who make city-life, first-job, student or relocation content.
  • Turn each partnership or event into a Reel, Story and community post.

What to measure

Instagram

Engagement that matters

Track reach, video views, average watch time, saves, shares, comments, profile visits, link clicks, DMs and enquiry volume.

LinkedIn

Trust and partnerships

Track impressions, meaningful comments, profile visits, follower growth, inbound partnership conversations and corporate or homeowner leads.

Recommended first 30 days

Start with 12 Reels: 4 property tours, 3 resident stories, 2 relatable new-city videos, 2 local guides and 1 community-event Reel. Support this with daily Stories, three LinkedIn posts each week and one local partnership or resident event. This creates enough content to learn which location, audience and format generates the best quality enquiries.

Prepared as a practical social-media and LinkedIn marketing plan for Shft-In. Any stated amenities, rates, referral benefits, availability, location information or service promises should be confirmed with the operations team before public use.
