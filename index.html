Marketing Executive Task · Reel Ideation

Make the move feel like a glow-up.

Instagram Reel concepts that position Shft-In as more than accommodation: a stylish, convenient and community-led place for young professionals, students and couples to live their city life.

Lifestyle-firstRelatable hooksTrend-friendly formatsConversion-aware CTA

Creative direction

The recurring message: moving cities should not mean compromising on comfort, aesthetic or connection. Each reel is designed for a 12–25 second vertical format, with a strong first-second hook, native text overlays and a save/share/comment prompt.

Execution note: use real Shft-In property footage, residents (with consent), team members and current amenity/service availability. Avoid promising amenities, locations or pricing that are not verified for the featured property.

10 Reel concepts + mini storyboards

01

“My PG vs My Shft-In Era”

Hook: “POV: you stopped calling it a PG.”

Before/after15–20 sec

Turn the familiar “struggle-to-upgrade” format into a visual lifestyle transformation.

2–5s: Beat-drop transition: door opens into a well-lit Shft-In space.

10

We Asked Residents: “What Feels Like Home?”

Hook: “A room is a room. But home is…”

UGC-style20–25 sec

Human-first brand storytelling that creates trust and repurposable testimonial content.

1
0–2s: Warm title over candid property footage.
2
2–16s: 4 residents complete the sentence in 2–3 word answers: “a safe landing,” “late-night chai,” “my own corner.”
3
16–21s: Visual proof behind each answer: room, shared space, friendly interaction.
4
21–25s: Brand end card: “Live your way. Find your Shft-In.”
Trust signal: retain natural pauses and real voices rather than over-polishing the clips.

Content system, not one-offs

  • Discovery: property tours, room reveals and value reels target active home seekers.
  • Consideration: routine, “what’s included” and moving-city reels answer practical questions.
  • Trust: resident interviews and UGC-style stories make the lifestyle credible.
  • Community: Bingo, roommate and friend-reaction formats invite comments, tags and shares.

Production checklist

  • Film vertical 9:16; keep the main subject clear of interface-safe zones.
  • Show the strongest visual in the first second—door reveal, reaction or room detail.
  • Use subtitles on every spoken line and keep on-screen text concise.
  • Make one hero shoot day yield 8–12 edit variations, each localised by property.
  • End with one clear action: DM, enquire, book a visit or tag a friend.

Recommended pilot: 3-reel launch set

Week 1: “My PG vs My Shft-In Era” for reach. Week 2: “What ₹___ Gets You Here” for leads. Week 3: “What Feels Like Home?” for trust. This sequence balances attention, consideration and credibility while giving Shft-In repeatable creative formats.
